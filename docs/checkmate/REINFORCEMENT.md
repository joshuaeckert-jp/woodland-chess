# Reinforcement mode

Reinforcement is the alternate opening for Project Checkmate. Full Army remains the default and keeps the existing pre-battle placement sequence.

## Opening state

Each side begins with its King on its home central square: Ivory on `e1`, Charcoal on `e8`. The remaining fifteen animals form that side's reserve hand. They are unit cards, not tactical cards: they do not come from the tactical deck, are never drawn automatically and do not use one of the three support slots.

The match immediately begins in battle. Both players still receive the selected five-card tactical opening hand and follow the normal draw rule.

## Deploying a reserve

Select or drag an animal card from the Animals hand onto an empty legal home square. A deployment costs the player's entire turn. It does not require a ready effect card and cannot be followed by a move, attack, support-card install, or printed effect during the same turn.

The legal zones are the same as Full Army placement:

- Kings and officers may enter either home rank.
- Rats may enter any of their first three ranks.
- A square must be empty and free of blocking terrain.

Cards dropped outside those zones, on an occupied square, into the opponent's territory, or into a support row return to the hand and cost nothing. Defeated animals never return to reserve.

## Tactical cards and victory

Tactical effect cards work unchanged. Each player still has three support slots; install, exhaust-to-order and burn actions remain one-action turns. The King uses the same HP and elimination victory rule as every other mode.

Reinforcement changes the opening economy, not combat geometry, card cooldowns, or the one-action rule. A saved match records its selected army mode and replays identically.
