# Shrine artwork — v0.8

The two transparent shrine backgrounds were created with the built-in `image_gen.imagegen` tool. Their texture matches the board and the approved Shrine Wings concept. Cards, labels, buttons, sockets and game rules remain live code; none are baked into the background images.

Saved assets:

- `public/assets/checkmate/shrine-vertical.webp` — 480 × 1440, with alpha; used for desktop side wings.
- `public/assets/checkmate/shrine-horizontal.webp` — 1170 × 390, with alpha; used for stacked phone/tablet shrines.

Generated PNGs were resized and encoded as WebP with Sharp; no original board or animal sprite was altered.

## Shared prompt

Game UI background asset for Woodland Chess. Reference image is STYLE ONLY: match its richly textured weathered grey-brown pixel stone shrines, moss vines, candles, and original board's detailed 2.5D pixel art. Do not render a screenshot, board, cards, card text, characters, buttons, labels or interface. Render ONLY a standalone miniature stone SHRINE as a transparent-background cutout, tightly composed. Sharp square pixel clusters, hand-painted pixel texture, muted earthy stone with small brass accents, clear projected top and front faces, convincing thickness, soft contained contact shadow. No scenic backdrop or floor. Empty recessed card lecterns have subtle carved leaf runes. Candles and moss are restrained so live cards placed over it remain the focus. Alpha transparency outside the asset.

## Vertical suffix

VERTICAL shrine wing, requested canvas 512x1536 portrait. Three identical portrait card recesses stacked vertically with centers exactly at 20%, 50%, and 80% of total height, each clear area about 68% of width and 24% of height. View from the same slightly elevated front perspective as the reference. Stone frame fills the tall narrow silhouette, little shelves between recesses, thicker bottom plinth. The three empty sockets must be spacious and flat dark enough to hold live card faces, with no obstructions. Keep silhouette and slots symmetric and aligned.

## Horizontal suffix

HORIZONTAL shrine for phone layout, requested canvas 1536x512 landscape. Three identical card sockets side by side, centers at 20%, 50%, 80% of total width. Thick wide masonry base with a raised stone top and obvious front thickness, modest side pillars/candles, richly chipped pixel stones, a few ivy strands. The actual empty sockets are dark flat areas on three low stone lecterns and occupy upper 70% of height; stone base occupies lower 30%. This is an extremely wide low stand, not a wall. Keep 3 unobstructed card resting positions. No rendered cards, only the stone asset.
